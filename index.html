Workshop on Field Studies and Competitive Gaming

FSCG - CHI 2027

Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania - May 2027

About the Workshop

This workshop explores methods for conducting field experiments in esports tournaments and other competitive gaming environments.

We are particularly interested in the tradeoffs between experimental control and studying competitive play in realistic settings involving factors such as spectators, pressure, environmental conditions, tournament structure, and player experience.
